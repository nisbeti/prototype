Keyboard shortcuts

Single keys work in English and on an Arabic keyboard, but not while you are typing in a box. Esc goes back to where you were.
On every page
Q ق
Go to Quran
B ك
Go to Books
R م
Go to Resources
A و
Go to Authors
M ن
Go to Publishers
L ل
Open the Library menu
↑ ↓
Move through an open menu
Reading a book
N ت
Next page
P س
Previous page
← →
Turn the page, the way the book runs: in an Arabic book the left arrow is the next page
C ف
Open the contents
R ق
Turn reading mode on or off
In reading mode
← →
Turn the page, the way the book runs
R ق Esc
Leave reading mode
Reading the Quran
Enter Space
Play the ayah, or open the tafsir, in focus
